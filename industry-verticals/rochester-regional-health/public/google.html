Steps to find a primary care doctor in Rochester

  • Search a provider directory: Rochester Regional Health lists family medicine doctors in Rochester, Victor and Wayne County, and marks who is accepting new patients.+1
  • Call a central scheduling line: many systems can match you with a PCP and book your first visit in one call. Rochester Regional Health's new-patient line is (585) 555-0100.1
  • Check your insurance: if you changed jobs or plans with the move, confirm the practice is in-network before booking.2
  • Transfer your records: sign a records-release form so your new office can request your history from your previous doctor.3
  • Ask neighbors: local forums often recommend practices close to where you live.4

Family medicine practices see adults and children, which is convenient for families who are new to the area.
